Peeling → GE pipeline — high-erasure regime (0.30 → 0.45)

PPVH erasure decoder (arXiv:2208.01002). 10000 trials per erasure rate, fixed RNG seed so all conditions decode the identical samples.

Pipeline under test: plain M=0 peeling, then a single global GF(2) Gaussian elimination over the residual Hz system (all remaining erased qubits at once) — no M=1, no M=2, no cluster-tree decomposition. The only optional step between peeling and GE is the DFS column reordering.
Baseline: the original paper decoder (full cascade — Peeling → VH → Cluster) with the only change being dense GE replaced by sparse GF(2) GE. The baseline uses no DFS.
GE time is elimination only; DFS reordering is measured separately and excluded from the GE-time chart. Times in milliseconds (GE/reorder are totals over all trials; decode time is the per-trial average).
The baseline reuses the seed-matched HIGHERASURE_ cascade data (identical samples; see the generator docstring).
